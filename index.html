Introduction To Me

I am a digital artist and a website developer.

What do I like to draw?

I enjoy making fanart, original art, sprites, pixel art, backgrounds, character designs, and much more!

What's my favorite thing to draw?

I love drawing cats and I love drawing half-capes! Cats are adorable and half-capes look really cool in my opinion! I also enjoy learning to draw new characters, such as learning to draw my own characters.

What are my least favorite things to draw?

I really dislike drawing hands. They're such a nightmare! So many little moving parts to them!!
